Glossary

Terms Definition
IP Address 🌐An IP address is a unique number that identifies a device on a network, like a street address for your computer on the internet or Wi-Fi
Packet 📦 A data packet is a small piece of information sent over a network. Big files are broken into packets so they can travel faster and be reassembled at the destination.
TCP 🔄 TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) is a rule that helps computers send data reliably over the internet, making sure it arrives correctly and in the right order.
UDP 🚀 UDP (User Datagram Protocol) is a fast way to send data over the internet without checking if it arrived correctly.
Firewall In a a network security device that monitors and filters incoming and outgoing network traffic based on an organization's previously established security policies.
LAN Stands for Local-Area Network. It's a collection of devices connected together in one physical location, such as a building, office, or home.
WAN Stands for Wide-Area Network. It's a collection of area networks or other networks that communite with one another.
